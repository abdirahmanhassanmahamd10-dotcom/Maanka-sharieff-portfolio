Case Study · UI/UX Design

Barbaarshe Kids®

A playful learning platform concept built around simple navigation, friendly visuals, and child-centered design.

RoleUI/UX Designer
Year2025
ToolsFigma · Illustrator
TypeProduct Design

Problem Statement

Children and families need a learning experience that feels simple, welcoming, and easy to navigate while still giving adults enough clarity to understand the product.

Objectives & Goals

Create a friendly visual language, reduce navigation friction, and make important learning actions easy to discover.

Research & User Needs

80%Clearer navigation
50%Faster discovery
30%More confidence
90%Visual appeal

Features & Functionalities

Simple learning paths, visual progress, clear categories, and playful interaction patterns designed around the needs of young learners.

Final Outcome

A structured product direction that turns research and user needs into a clear, friendly learning experience.
